## Description
Never lose a client to forgetfulness again.

Nudge is the smartest follow-up app for freelancers, consultants, and small business owners. 80% of deals close after the 5th touchpoint, but most people quit after the 2nd. Nudge makes sure you never quit.

SMART FOLLOW-UP REMINDERS
Set your cadence and Nudge automatically reminds you when it's time to reach out. No more sticky notes, no more forgotten clients.

ONE-TAP ACTIONS
Call, email, or text your clients directly from the app. Every interaction is logged automatically. No manual data entry.

AI-POWERED SUGGESTIONS
Get smart follow-up recommendations based on your interaction history. AI drafts email templates so you can respond in seconds.

YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
See today's follow-ups, overdue clients, and upcoming tasks in one clean dashboard. Start every day knowing exactly who to contact.

CLIENT MANAGEMENT MADE SIMPLE
Import contacts from your phone. Organize clients with tags and notes. Track every call, meeting, and email in one place.

FREE FOREVER
Manage up to 25 clients for free. No credit card required. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited clients, AI features, and cloud sync.

WHY NUDGE?

- One-tap actions with auto-logging
- AI follow-up suggestions and email drafts
- Works completely offline

Perfect for freelancers, insurance agents, real estate agents, consultants, and anyone who values client relationships.
