Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new client?
Tap the + button on the Clients tab, fill in the client details, and tap Save. You can also import contacts from your iPhone.
How do follow-up reminders work?
When you add a client, Nudge automatically schedules follow-ups based on your chosen cadence (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly). You'll receive a notification when it's time to follow up.
How do I manage my subscription?
Go to Settings > Your Name > Subscriptions on your iPhone, or use the "Restore Purchases" option in Nudge's Settings.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes! You can cancel anytime through your Apple ID subscription settings. You'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.
How do I import contacts?
Go to the Clients tab, tap the menu button (...), and select "Import Contacts." Nudge will request permission to access your contacts.
Is my data safe?
Yes! All your data is stored locally on your device. If you enable Cloud Sync, data is stored in your personal iCloud account. We never access or share your data.
What's the difference between Free and Pro?
The Free tier supports up to 25 clients with basic features. Pro unlocks unlimited clients, AI follow-up suggestions, email drafts, cloud sync, widgets, and more.

Subscription Management

To manage or cancel your Nudge subscription:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  • Tap your name at the top
  • Tap Subscriptions
  • Find and tap Nudge
  • Choose to cancel or change your subscription
