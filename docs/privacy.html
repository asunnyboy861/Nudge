Privacy Policy for Nudge

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Information We Collect

Nudge is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary to provide our services:

  • Client Data: Contact information you voluntarily enter (names, emails, phone numbers, company names). This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Follow-Up Data: Reminders, notes, and interaction logs you create. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Usage Data: We do not collect, track, or transmit your usage analytics.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the Nudge app functionality:

  • To manage your client relationships and follow-up reminders
  • To send you local notifications for scheduled follow-ups
  • To sync data across your devices via iCloud (if you enable Cloud Sync)

Data Storage

  • Local Storage: All data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData.
  • Cloud Sync (Optional): If you enable Cloud Sync, data is stored in your personal iCloud account via CloudKit. We cannot access your CloudKit data.
  • No Third-Party Servers: We do not store your data on any third-party servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your data remains on your device and in your personal iCloud account (if sync is enabled).

Notifications

Nudge requests permission to send local push notifications for follow-up reminders. You can manage notification preferences in your device Settings or within the app.

Contacts Access

Nudge may request access to your device contacts to import client information. This access is optional and only used when you explicitly choose to import contacts. We do not transmit your contacts data anywhere.

In-App Purchases

Subscription payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive only a transaction confirmation, not your payment details.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • You can delete any data at any time within the app
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data
  • CloudKit data can be managed through your iCloud settings

Children's Privacy

Nudge is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
